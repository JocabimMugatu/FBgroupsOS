The methodology

Embedded participation,
not advertising.

Group Reach maps the communities where your ICP already gathers, participates with genuinely useful content as a credible voice in the category, monitors every thread for buying signals, and hands warm context to your sales team — not cold lists.
01
Group Intelligence & Mapping
We identify and score every Facebook group where your ICP is active — by size, engagement quality, posting norms, and admin tolerance. You get a ranked map of where real buyer conversations are happening before we engage.
02
Value-First Community Participation
We participate as a credible practitioner, not a vendor. Content leads with genuine insight, useful workflows, and category expertise. No pitch, no product mention until trust is established. Authority in the feed first.
03
Intent Signal Monitoring
We monitor every thread for buying signals — pain points your product solves, competitor comparisons, workflow complaints, tool recommendation requests. Every high-intent post is flagged and routed within 24 hours.
04
Context-Rich Warm Handoff
When someone shows clear intent, we prepare a context-rich handoff: the original post, the pain point, why it matters, and the recommended next step. Your team can reply publicly, send a thoughtful message, or route the account into your CRM.
05
Monthly Market Intelligence Report
Every month you receive a structured report: top pain points, competitor mentions, sentiment trends, content that drove engagement, and pipeline influenced. Your community becomes a standing source of market intelligence.
Built for trust-protected communities

Community participation
without the sketchy stuff.

Private groups work because trust is protected. Group Reach does not use fake accounts, fake testimonials, spam blasts, or undisclosed shilling. Every engagement is authentic, client-approved, and grounded in real expertise.

Before participating in any group, we map its rules, posting norms, admin tolerance, and commercial risk. If a group doesn't allow promotional content, we can still monitor it for market intelligence without forcing engagement that could damage your brand.

The goal is to make your brand a genuinely useful voice in the category — so that when a buyer is ready to evaluate, you're already a name they recognize and trust.
