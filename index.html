The High-Touch Deficit

Ads are transactional. SaaS is a relationship.

You can buy your way to the top of the feed, but well-funded startups face a different problem: proving complex value over time. Community solves this.

👀

The "Single Touch" Fallacy

B2B buyers require 7-13 touches before trusting a software vendor. Trying to force a complex workflow demo into a single Meta ad click skyrockets your CAC and yields low-intent leads.

🧩

Demonstrating Varying Use-Cases

A static landing page can't speak to every niche. A Facebook group allows you to incubate leads by showing them micro-playbooks tailored exactly to their industry over several weeks.

🛡️

Combating Churn

Community members stick around. By nurturing an environment where users help each other troubleshoot and share wins, your software becomes an integrated ecosystem, drastically raising LTV.
