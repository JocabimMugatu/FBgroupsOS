The methodology

Infiltration,
not broadcasting.

01
Group Intelligence & Mapping
We identify every Facebook group where your ICP is active — sorted by size, engagement quality, and posting rules. You get a ranked map of where the real conversations are happening before we touch anything.
02
Value-First Content Infiltration
We post as a credible participant, not a vendor. Every piece of content leads with a genuine insight, workflow, or opinion — no pitch, no product mention. We build authority in the feed before anything commercial happens.
03
Intent Signal Monitoring
We watch every thread for buying signals — complaints about a competitor, questions about a workflow your product solves, frustration with their current stack. Those members get flagged for DM outreach within 24 hours.
04
Warm DM Pipeline Handoff
We never pitch cold in the comments. Every high-intent member moves from public thread to a private, contextual DM — referencing their exact post. By the time they hit your AE, they already know you. Close rates run 40%+ above cold outbound.
