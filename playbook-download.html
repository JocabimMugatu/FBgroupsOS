The Community-Led Growth Playbook

How B2B SaaS companies are moving away from rented Meta ads to owned, high-intent Facebook communities.

01
Build The Honeypot
We don't brand the group around your SaaS product. We brand it around the problem your ICP faces. If you sell recruitment tech, the group is "Technical Recruiting Masters." We optimize the name for Facebook's native search engine to capture organic founder intent.
02
Seed & Scale with Hyper-Targeted Offers
Instead of running Meta ads to a high-friction landing page, we run ads directly to the private Facebook group. We seed the initial engagement with hyper-tailored offers. (e.g., "Giving away 3 lifetime licenses to our software for bootstrapped founders who join this week.")
03
The "Sam Parr" Value Loop
We manage the day-to-day moderation, ensuring zero spam. We spark conversations that allow your product to be naturally positioned as the solution. When a member asks a question, your existing evangelists (and our moderators) step in to recommend your tool natively.
04
The Pipeline Handoff
When a member expresses high intent, we move the conversation to private DMs. We qualify the founder and hand off a warm, heavily-nurtured lead directly to your AE's calendar.
